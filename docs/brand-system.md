# Lumière Index identity

16 November 2026. The founder supplied a compact news-site reference and selected tomato orange. The design uses an original rectangular LI monogram, Arial typography for navigation/headlines, and Georgia for long article text. It does not reproduce USA Today marks or import celebrity material from the reference.

- Signature tomato: #FF4F32. Use for large brand surfaces, rules and the mark.
- Accessible accent: #B92C16. Use for small orange text on white; white on the signature orange is decorative logo artwork, not body copy.
- Ink: #191C1C. Paper: #FFFFFF. Secondary surface: #E9EDE7.
- Logo files live in public/brand: wordmark.svg, wordmark-light.svg, monogram.svg, and transparent PNG exports. Keep clear space of at least the width of the I stroke; do not stretch the mark.
- Full navigation: Latest, Celebrity, Film & TV, Music, Culture, Exclusives, Features.
- Lead photography and numbered story controls use published CMS stories. No decorative stock people or invented headlines are seeded for launch.
- Opening identity animation lasts 750ms on document load, never intercepts clicks, and is removed entirely for reduced-motion preferences. Client-side navigation does not replay it.
- Responsive layouts: compact desktop navigation; menu at 900px and below; four-column supporting stories become two columns; long-form article pages retain a narrow reading measure.
